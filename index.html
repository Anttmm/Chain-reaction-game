Launch your orb!
Pulses: 12
Drag BACK from the glowing orb at the bottom to launch. During play, touch ANYWHERE and drag in the direction you want the ORB to fly. Releasing redirects and shoots the orb itself — there is no invisible beam. When the orb actually hits a creature, THAT impact creates the wide pulse and can kick nearby creatures into a chain reaction. Pink → orange → blue → yellow → green → purple → red. Matching colors merge whenever they touch. The arena keeps more creatures in play so big combo moments stay possible. Bigger creatures are only slightly heavier, so even late-tier creatures can still get launched around. Tap once to enable sound on iPhone.
