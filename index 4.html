Drag BACK from the glowing orb at the bottom to launch. During play, touch ANYWHERE, drag in the direction you want the orb to fly, and release. Your shot is the ORB itself now — there is no invisible beam. The big pulse happens only when the orb physically hits a ball. The white orb is heavy now, so it pushes through hits instead of bouncing backward. Balls directly in front of the struck ball are protected from the impact pulse, so the rear ball can actually catch them and merge. Impact pulses have a longer range, the board keeps more balls available for chains, and bigger balls are only slightly heavier. Pink → orange → blue → yellow → green → purple → red. Matching colors merge whenever they touch. Tap once to enable sound on iPhone.
